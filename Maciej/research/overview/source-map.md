## Superseded advice and unresolved facts

Do not carry forward optional live semantics, permissive outage behavior, a second provider URL in editable policy, fine-tuning, automatic eight-case threshold selection, zero-false-positive promises, a 100–200 ms gateway promise, or benchmarking with production safeguards disabled. Current policy has three fixed risk questions and mandatory live Laya. The reference workflow is controlled chat/import/public export; email/outbox is deferred.

Priority findings are semantic usage under-reservation, runtime revision-variable mapping, parser metadata and canonical text gaps, feed ownership, bridge recovery/retention configuration, and a development-label ambiguity. Each has an owner and recommendation in the [decision register](../integration/contracts-and-open-decisions.md).

## Verification boundary

Research completion means the briefs are sourced, mutually linked, mapped to tests and explicit about missing evidence. It does not satisfy G1/G2 or any live acceptance gate. Repository checks validate documentation/starter consistency only. Record final commands and outcomes in the research commit handoff; keep service versions, dataset checksums and genuine future run results separate from recommendations.
